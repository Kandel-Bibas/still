In the code

A tap exists only when an app is muted, quieter than 100%, or pinned to a device.

Everything else routes straight to the hardware, untouched. That is why the purple recording dot in your menu bar comes and goes: it tracks live taps, not whether Still is running.

Four limits are worth knowing before you rely on it.

  • Stereo tap to a single float output only. Encoded and exclusive-device audio are unsupported.
  • A brand-new process may be audible for a moment before discovery creates its tap.
  • Bluetooth changes and sleep/wake can produce a brief gap.
  • No boost, EQ, solo, ducking, hotkeys or per-tab routing.
